# App Store copy — version 1.1.0 (build 10)

## Promotional text

Explore the MLP Video Library by language and format, then turn lessons into action with private reflections, field plans, and progress.

## Description

MLP Video Library brings Marketplace Literacy learning resources together in English, French, Hindi, Spanish, Swahili, and Telugu. Choose a language, browse resource formats, and find the lesson or visual story you need.

Each lesson can become a practical step. Record a useful idea and the next action you will try, then mark the lesson complete. Follow a guided 12-step learning path, choose a daily goal, and use the Field Workbook to plan a customer conversation, offer, price test, and target date.

Save lessons and track completed lessons, learning streaks, field tests, and recent activity. Search the library by language, format, title, and topic.

Your learning path, reflections, field plans, saved lessons, and progress are stored privately on your device. After the catalog loads once, lesson details and your saved work remain available offline. Video playback requires an internet connection.

No sign-in, subscription, or in-app purchase is required.

## What's New

The familiar MLP Video Library is back at the center of the iPhone app. Browse by language and resource format, search the catalog, and keep using guided lessons, private reflections, the Field Workbook, saved resources, and progress tracking.
