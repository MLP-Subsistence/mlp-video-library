# App Review Notes — Marketplace Literacy 1.1.0 (Build 10)

Thank you for reviewing this update. The app remains a native SwiftUI learning and field-practice app. This update restores the recognizable MLP Video Library language and resource-format organization while retaining the guided learning, reflection, workbook, progress, and offline capabilities reviewed in build 9. It does not embed or navigate the Marketplace Literacy website. WebKit is used only for inline YouTube playback within a native lesson detail.

## Suggested review path

1. Open **Library**. The home screen shows the original Marketplace Literacy branding and language artwork, plus a visible **Continue your learning path** entry. Choose **English → Image Diaries → a lesson** to browse the catalog natively.
2. In the lesson detail, use **Turn this lesson into action**. Enter a useful idea and a next action; both are required before the lesson can be completed. Save the lesson if desired.
3. Open **Practice** to choose a learning language and daily goal, follow the 12-step path, and continue to **Field Workbook**. Create a field plan with a customer, need, offer, price test, interview question, next action, and target date.
4. Open **Saved** to see bookmarked lessons and reach **Progress**, including completed lessons, daily goals, streaks, field tests, and completion history. **Search** provides native language, format, and text filters.

Reflections, field plans, saved lessons, progress, and the cached catalog persist on device. Reflections and field plans remain available offline. Videos require a network connection. No sign-in is required for the public review path.
